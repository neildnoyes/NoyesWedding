Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code?
Think semi-formal. For men, dress pants with a button-down and tie (or a full suit if you prefer). For women, a knee-length or longer dress is perfect.


Is the ceremony/reception held indoors or outdoors?
All events will be outdoors at various locations around the venue.


Can I bring a guest?
We kindly ask that only the guests who were individually invited attend. While spouses and significant others are included where noted, we're not able to accomodate extra guests beyond those planned for.


When can I arrive at the venue?
Those not opting into the provided transportation can plan to arrive at 4:30 PM.


Will transportation be provided?
The Lowcountry Trolley will be arriving at Hyatt Place Charleston/Historic District at 4:00 PM to provide transportation to the venue. The same trolley will be at the venue at 10:00 PM to make its way back into town with a dropoff location of Hyatt Place.


What if I have a food related allergy?
Please let us know in the comments section of your RSVP if you have any food related allergies.
