Accommodations & Travel

We’re thrilled to celebrate at the Legare Waring House — a historic estate within Charles Towne Landing State Historic Site, surrounded by majestic oak trees and reflecting lagoons.

Recommended hotels nearby include the Charleston Place, The Dewberry, and Hotel Emeline. Charleston International Airport (CHS) is about 15 minutes away.
